File format


Do not deliver assignments as zip files, but instead upload files individually. Uploading zip files makes the grading process more difficult, and might cause your submission to recieve a failed grade.


 

Groups

You may submit assignments in groups of 1 or 2. To sign up for a group, first click "Assignment X Group Sign-Up Sheet", and then click the group you want to join. It is not possible to leave a group once you have joined, so please be careful when joining. After joining a group, the group assignment delivery should be visible.

Each group is only used for a single assignment. If you want to do another assignment with the same person, you both need to sign up for a new group associated with the new assignment.

IMPORTANT: Both members must be in the group before submitting. Only group members that are in the group at the time of submission will recieve a grade. If you are unsure whether both members were in the group when you submitted, please resubmit.

Exam Info

General information


Information about the time and location of the exam can be found on StudWeb and the "about page"
Links to an external site.
, but we also provide information here for your convenience. In the case of conflicting information, the information on this page should be considered out of date.


 

Exams at NTNU are organized centrally, this means that as course administrators we cannot change the date or location of the exam.

 

Note that there may be multiple exam locations, you need to check StudWeb to find the location assigned to you.


 

Please see NTNU's help page regarding exams and NTNU's FAQ page regarding exams for more general information.

 

Details

Examination arrangement:   School exam, 4 hours, written. 
Date:  07.12.2026
Exam language: Questions only in English, you can answer in English or Norwegian
Examination aids:  D. None.
Grade: Letter grades based 100% on final exam. 3 mandatory assignments must be delivered and passed ("godkjent") in order to take the exam. We will use the percentage grading scale.
